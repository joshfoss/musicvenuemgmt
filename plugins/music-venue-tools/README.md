# music-venue-tools

Find, verify, research and rate live music venues for your band or solo act.

## What's included

- **band-venue-fit:** rates venues A to X for your act, with a written reason for each grade. Works for one venue ("Would my band fit Gruene Hall?"), an area ("Rate the Hays County venues for my band") or your own list. Includes a venue list template.
- **live-music-check:** answers "does this place have live music?" with evidence, and gates the ratings.
- **venue-research:** finds what each venue books, how, and who to contact.
- **venue-dataset:** reads and updates the shared venue master.
- **Venue Master connector:** the shared, live list of verified live music venue leads (about 790 Central Texas venues to start). Confirmed findings from every user improve it for everyone.

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin, then check the Venue Master connector is enabled on the plugin's Connectors page.
2. Ask, for example: "Rate the Travis County venues for my band."
3. The first time, Claude asks about your act: name, number of performers, genres, covers or originals, and your home zip code. It saves this so you're only asked once.

To rate your own list, ask Claude for the venue list template, fill in BUSINESS NAME, CITY and STATE for each venue, and attach it.

## What's shared and what stays private

- **Shared:** facts about venues (live music evidence, genres, act size, admission, booking contacts published by the venue, open or closed). Everything you or Claude submits is checked automatically and logged; closures wait for the maintainer's review.
- **Private (stays in your own workspace):** your act profile, home address or zip, fit ratings, bookings and rates. The shared master rejects this kind of data.

## Notes

- Claude reads venue websites while researching; your app may ask you to approve each new site.
- The master covers Central Texas today. Venues you confirm in other areas are added as you go.
- Download the current list any time: https://venue-master.jfoss.workers.dev/export?state=TX
